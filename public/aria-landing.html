Capabilities

Built to think,
not just answer.

Six things ARIA does that most assistants can't — or won't.

Reasoning over recall

ARIA works through problems in multi-step chains. She doesn't pattern-match — she actually thinks before she speaks.

Persistent memory

She remembers what you said three weeks ago, and connects it to what you're saying now. Context isn't lost — it compounds.

Honest disagreement

If your idea has a hole, she'll name it. ARIA isn't built to flatter — she's built to make your thinking sharper.

Adaptive tone

She reads the room. Quick and direct when you're in a rush, slow and reflective when the question deserves it.

Cross-session continuity

Every conversation is a chapter, not an episode. Threads pick up exactly where they left off — no re-explaining.

Private by design

Your conversations aren't training data. They're yours. Local-first encryption, zero retention, no exceptions.

About ARIA

An assistant should challenge you, not applaud you.

ARIA was built on a simple premise: most assistants are designed to be useful. Few are designed to be honest. The result is a generation of tools that agree, summarize, and pacify — and a generation of users who quietly stop thinking in their presence.

We wanted something different. An assistant that holds the tension of a hard question long enough to actually answer it. One that remembers the shape of your thinking, not just the words. One that, when you say something weak, has the nerve to say so.

ARIA is not a chatbot. She's a partner. She'll push back when you need it, hold context when you forget, and tell you the truth when it would be easier not to.
